# Disabled-user test: 2026-10-31

All personas were simulated in headless Chromium (Playwright). Keyboard and voice input were scripted. "Screen reader" means the accessibility tree (`ariaSnapshot`) plus text recorded from live regions. No real screen reader or speech recognition was used. I tested the working tree as it stood during the run. Another agent was editing `pawthfinder/*.js`, `pawthfinder.css` and `account/account.js` at the same time, so line numbers below are approximate.

## Summary
The scripted harness (`npm run test:personas`) passes: Alex, Sam, Riley and Morgan all finish booking, with 0 blockers, 0 major and 1 minor. Exploring past the happy path, every persona could still finish their task, but with real difficulty in several places. The worst problems:
- **Mismatched names.** Once Translate is on (the keyboard and voice presets turn it on by default), step instructions still use the portal's old names, such as "Encounter Mgmt", but the screen now shows the plain-language ones, such as "Appointments". Voice control can't find the button the dog names.
- **Lost focus.** Keyboard focus drops to the page body whenever a notice is dismissed or one of its buttons is used.
- **Silent sign-out.** When the portal signs the user out, a screen-reader user is never told why.
- **Lost sign-up progress.** Reloading the sign-up page erases everything typed so far.
- **400% zoom.** The current step and the speech bubble are cut off or covered.

There is no earlier dated findings report, so I can't show a trend. Against `latest.md`, exploring found 7 major and 10 minor issues that the scripted run doesn't cover.

## Fix these next
1. Build step instructions from the control's current visible name, not hard-coded portal jargon, so they match what Translate shows (pawthfinder.js `buildSteps`, ~L211–250).
2. After a notice is dismissed, or a notice button or the "fill" command is used, put focus somewhere sensible (pawthfinder.js `renderNotices` / notices click handler ~L528–546, `previewFill` ~L1108).
3. Announce the portal sign-out in the live region, including after a rest stop (pawthfinder.js `onStepChange` ~L658, `resume` ~L1356).
4. Keep sign-up answers (except the password) in sessionStorage so a reload or interruption doesn't erase them (account/account.js `show()` / `draft`).
5. Accept natural voice commands that name the control ("press sign in", "choose Dr. Patel") (pawthfinder.js `handleCommand` ~L1366, `chooseIt` ~L728).

## What worked well
- **Sign-up and sign-in errors are good.** Empty submit moves focus to an alert, "3 things need fixing", with links to each field. Each link focuses its field. `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` are set. A wrong password gets a clear, non-blaming message.
- **The session-start picker works.** Focus starts inside it and Tab stays trapped. Each tile announces "…: picked". Account choices are pre-picked when you press Start.
- **The keyboard repairs work.** Alex booked the whole appointment without Alt+Shift+N: real labels ("Type of care", "Doctor", "Reason for visit"), mouse-only buttons that become buttons and links, a robot check that becomes `checkbox "I am not a robot"`, and a focus-follow that announced "Step N of 17".
- **Hidden time limit (intended portal barrier) is handled.** The warning comes early, "Keep me signed in" works ("I kept you signed in."), and answers are offered back after a timeout (harness).
- **The consent box is never ticked for the user**, and Fetch says it is skipping it ("✋ Skipping: The payment agreement — that’s your decision.").
- **Big mode works for Taylor.** In "Bigger text and buttons" mode, portal radios and checkboxes grow from 10×10 to 26×26 and nav links to 60px tall. Off-center clicks of 6–7px then hit, and clicking a radio's label text works. All Pawthfinder controls were ≥24px even in default mode, and the notice ✕ is 36px (44px in big mode).
- **Account pages fit at 200% and 400%** with no sideways scroll, including big text plus strong contrast.
- **Collapsing the panel (Alt+Shift+H) at 400%** leaves the bubble fully readable.

## Not tested / limits
- No real screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver). Announcements are inferred from live-region DOM changes, so timing and interruption behaviour of `role=alert` vs `status` were not heard.
- No real speech recognition or voice-control software. "Voice control" was approximated by `getByRole(name)` matching, and Pawthfinder speech by typing into `#pf-cmd`. With a real microphone the command box being hidden on the Fetch tab wouldn't matter to Sam, so it is reported only as a keyboard focus issue (F2).
- Chromium only, headless, Linux. Zoom was simulated by viewport size (683px and 342px), not browser zoom. Text spacing and Windows high-contrast/forced-colors were not tested.
- I didn't fully explore the Refill and Test-results goals, the Speak up letter/card, or the Download button.
- Source files changed while I was testing (another agent was editing), so a few findings may already have shifted. All evidence is from this run. Scratch scripts are in `tests/scratch/`.
